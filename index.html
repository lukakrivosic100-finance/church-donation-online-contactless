Gift Aid

Gift Aid may allow an eligible UK charity to claim an extra 25p for every £1 donated by a qualifying UK taxpayer, at no extra cost to the donor.

Example wording only: “I am a UK taxpayer and understand that if I pay less Income Tax and/or Capital Gains Tax in the current tax year than the amount of Gift Aid claimed on all my donations, it is my responsibility to pay the difference.” A real Gift Aid declaration must name the eligible charity and include the donor's full name and home address. The charity's eligibility and approved wording must be confirmed before this feature is used for real donations.

Important information

This is not a live donation service. SMS verification sends your mobile number and a one-time code request to TextBee; verification records are stored temporarily in Supabase. No card details are collected, no payment is taken and donation details are not saved. Before going live, the organisation must confirm its legal charity name and Gift Aid eligibility, privacy notice, payment provider and donation terms.
